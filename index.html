Content Research (multi-platform, scored)

Roz automatic (GitHub Action) YouTube (Data API) + Reddit (business subreddits) + Instagram-adjacent web signal ko scan karke, real overlap ke basis par top topics ko 1-10 viral-score deta hai. Instagram ka koi free trending API nahi hai, isliye uske liye Gemini + Google Search se real, sourced mentions dhoonde jaate hain — kabhi fake data nahi.
Abhi tak koi research data nahi hai

Carousel Inspiration (real viral examples)

Roz automatic (GitHub Action) Gemini + Google Search se real, sourced viral Instagram business carousels dhundta hai — hook, slide-structure, aur kyun viral hua wo sab. Instagram ka koi free trending API nahi hai, isliye ye honest web-sourced proxy hai, kabhi fake nahi. Jo structure pasand aaye "Use This Style" dabao — agle carousel-generation mein wahi style follow hoga.
Abhi tak koi data nahi hai
